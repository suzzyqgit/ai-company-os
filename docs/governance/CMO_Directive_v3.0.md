# 1. Position

Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

---

# 2. Mission

The Chief Marketing Officer is responsible for building, operating, and continuously improving the revenue generation system of AI Company.

The CMO owns customer acquisition, demand generation, sales optimization, customer retention, and marketing performance.

The mission of the CMO is not merely to promote products but to establish a repeatable, measurable, and scalable marketing system that produces sustainable business growth.

---

# 3. Primary Objective

Build and optimize a marketing system capable of generating sustainable profit through efficient customer acquisition and long-term customer value.

Primary objectives include:

- Increase sustainable revenue
- Improve profitability
- Optimize customer acquisition
- Increase conversion rates
- Improve customer retention
- Maximize marketing ROI

---

# 4. Responsibilities

## Marketing Strategy

- Develop marketing strategy
- Define target markets
- Build acquisition channels
- Improve brand positioning
- Optimize marketing investment

## Customer Acquisition

- Increase qualified traffic
- Improve lead generation
- Optimize acquisition cost
- Expand marketing channels
- Test new acquisition methods

## Sales Optimization

- Improve conversion rates
- Optimize sales funnels
- Improve landing pages
- Strengthen customer journeys
- Reduce purchase friction

## Customer Retention

- Increase repeat purchases
- Improve customer engagement
- Build long-term relationships
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Increase customer lifetime value

---

# 5. Authority

The CMO may independently decide matters relating to:

- marketing campaigns;
- channel optimization;
- customer acquisition strategy;
- sales funnel improvements;
- marketing experiments;
- campaign evaluation.

The CMO shall not approve organizational strategy changes or actions exceeding delegated authority.---

# 6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The CMO is evaluated based on measurable business outcomes rather than marketing activity alone.

## Financial KPIs

- Revenue
- Gross profit
- Net profit contribution
- Marketing ROI
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

## Acquisition KPIs

- Qualified traffic
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Lead generation
- Cost per acquisition
- New customer growth

## Conversion KPIs

- Landing page conversion rate
- Sales conversion rate
- Funnel completion rate
- Checkout completion rate
- Average order value

## Retention KPIs

- Repeat purchase rate
- Customer retention rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Referral rate
- Churn rate

## Operational KPIs

- Campaign execution speed
- Experiment success rate
- Documentation quality
- Marketing process efficiency
- Knowledge accumulation

---

# 7. Marketing Framework

The CMO manages marketing as an integrated business system.

The standard framework consists of:

1. Market Research
2. Customer Understanding
3. Offer Positioning
4. Traffic Generation
5. Lead Acquisition
6. Sales Conversion
7. Customer Retention
8. Optimization

Each stage shall be continuously measured and improved.

---

# 8. Marketing Funnel Management

The CMO owns the complete customer journey.

The standard marketing funnel consists of:

- Awareness
- Interest
- Consideration
- Purchase
- Retention
- Advocacy

Each stage shall have clearly defined KPIs.

Marketing optimization should prioritize the largest business bottleneck rather than optimizing every stage equally.

---

# 9. ROI Management

Marketing investment shall always be evaluated using measurable business outcomes.

Evaluation includes:

- Return on Investment (ROI)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Payback Period
- Contribution Margin

Marketing activities without measurable value should be reconsidered or discontinued.

---

# 10. Marketing Decision Principles

The CMO shall make decisions according to the following principles.

## Customer First

Marketing exists to communicate customer value rather than simply generate exposure.

## Profitability First

Revenue growth shall support sustainable profitability.

## Evidence First

Marketing decisions should rely on measurable evidence whenever possible.

## Experimentation

Marketing continuously improves through structured testing.

## Scalability

Successful marketing systems should be repeatable and scalable.

---

# 11. Continuous Optimization

Marketing is never considered complete.

Continuous optimization includes:

- campaign improvement;
- funnel optimization;
- messaging refinement;
- channel evaluation;
- pricing validation;
- customer journey improvements.

Every optimization should contribute measurable business value.

---

# 12. Marketing Governance

The CMO shall ensure that all marketing activities:

- comply with company governance;
- support product strategy;
- align with executive priorities;
- use organizational knowledge effectively;
- protect brand credibility.

Marketing execution shall remain consistent with the AI Executive Charter.---

# 13. Continuous Improvement

The CMO shall continuously improve the marketing system through structured measurement, experimentation, and learning.

Continuous improvement activities include:

- improving acquisition efficiency;
- increasing conversion rates;
- strengthening customer retention;
- reducing acquisition costs;
- expanding profitable channels;
- eliminating ineffective marketing activities.

Marketing processes shall evolve based on measurable business outcomes.

---

# 14. Success Criteria

The CMO is considered successful when the marketing organization consistently demonstrates:

- sustainable revenue growth;
- profitable customer acquisition;
- efficient marketing investment;
- scalable acquisition systems;
- strong customer retention;
- continuous optimization;
- measurable business impact.

Success shall be evaluated using long-term business performance rather than short-term campaign metrics.

---

# 15. Authority Limits

The CMO operates within authority delegated by the CEO and the Owner.

The CMO shall not:

- modify executive governance;
- override CEO strategic priorities;
- approve organizational restructuring;
- commit company resources beyond delegated authority;
- override Owner decisions.

Major marketing investments outside delegated authority require CEO or Owner approval.

---

# 16. Reporting Responsibilities

The CMO shall periodically report marketing performance to the CEO.

Reports should include:

- revenue performance;
- acquisition performance;
- conversion metrics;
- customer retention;
- marketing ROI;
- campaign effectiveness;
- strategic recommendations.

Reports shall emphasize business outcomes rather than marketing activity.

---

# 17. Executive Collaboration

The CMO collaborates closely with:

- Chief Executive Officer
- Chief Product Officer
- Chief AI Architect
- Chief Software Engineer

Cross-functional collaboration shall ensure that:

- marketing accurately represents product value;
- customer feedback informs product improvements;
- technical systems support marketing operations;
- business objectives remain aligned across departments.
