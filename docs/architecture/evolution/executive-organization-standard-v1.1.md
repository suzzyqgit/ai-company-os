# Executive Organization Standard v1.1

## Purpose

This standard defines the official executive organization of AI Company OS and
distinguishes active executives from future planned roles.

---

# Executive Classification

## Active Executive Roles

These roles are officially established.

- Owner
- CEO
- Chief AI Architect
- Chief Software Engineer
- Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
- Chief Product Officer (CPO)

---

## Planned Executive Roles

These roles are defined as future organizational extensions.

- Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Status:

- Planned
- Not Active
- No operational authority until officially established by CEO.

---

# Placeholder Rule

Planned executives may appear in architecture documents only when:

- expressing future architecture;
- defining extension points;
- maintaining consistency of organization models.

They must never be interpreted as active authorities.

---

# Governance

Changes to executive classification require CEO approval.

Future executive additions shall be managed through ADR and Change Management Framework.
