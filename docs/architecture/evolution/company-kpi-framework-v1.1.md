# Company KPI Framework v1.1

## Purpose

This framework standardizes KPI management across AI Company OS.

---

# KPI Hierarchy

## Level 1

Company KPI

Purpose:

Evaluate overall business performance.

Examples

- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Operating Margin
- ROI
- Business Growth
- Customer Satisfaction

---

## Level 2

Domain KPI

Each Executive Domain owns its KPI.

Examples

CMO

- Traffic
- Conversion Rate
- CAC
- Marketing ROI

CPO

- Product Success Rate
- Customer Adoption
- Product ROI

Chief AI Architect

- Architecture Coverage
- Documentation Quality
- Repository Consistency

Chief Software Engineer

- Deployment Success
- Test Coverage
- Lead Time
- Defect Rate

---

## Level 3

Operational KPI

Operational metrics supporting Domain KPIs.

Examples

- Daily Tasks Completed
- ADR Created
- Repository Validation Rate
- Import Success Rate
- Build Success
- Documentation Review Time

---

# Governance

Operational KPI rolls up into Domain KPI.

Domain KPI rolls up into Company KPI.

No KPI may exist without an owning Executive.

---

# Review Cycle

Operational KPI

Weekly

Domain KPI

Monthly

Company KPI

Quarterly
