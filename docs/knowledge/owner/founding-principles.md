# Founding Principles

## Purpose

Preserve the long-term principles that define AI Company OS.

## Principles

### Repository First

Repository is the Single Source of Truth.

---

### Business First

Technology exists to improve business outcomes.

---

### Profit First

Profitability is the highest operational KPI.

---

### Human for Judgment

Humans retain responsibility for final judgment.

---

### AI for Execution

AI should automate repeatable work whenever possible.

---

## Change Policy

These principles should change only through CEO approval.
