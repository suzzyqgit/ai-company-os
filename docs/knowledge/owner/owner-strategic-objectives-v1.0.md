# KAVORA Owner Strategic Objectives v1.0

## Purpose

Preserve Owner-confirmed strategic objectives, constraints, and decision preferences required for durable KAVORA executive decision continuity.

This artifact is normalized business knowledge. It does not preserve the interview transcript, private-life details, or personal information that is not required for KAVORA decisions.

This artifact does not amend Role Profiles, governance, architecture, authority boundaries, or AI Company OS standards.

## 1. Primary Economic Objective

### Initial Milestone

Reach monthly net profit of at least JPY 1,000,000 within 12 months of the 2026-08-12 Owner Interview.

Target horizon: 2027-08-12.

### Stability Milestone

Maintain monthly net profit of JPY 1,000,000 to JPY 2,000,000 for 6 to 12 consecutive months.

### Long-Term Scale Principle

No fixed long-term profit ceiling is defined.

After stable profitability is established, KAVORA should continue to scale when the expected increase in profit is rational and does not materially undermine business stability.

## 2. Normalized Decision Principle

The Owner's strategic priorities are normalized as follows:

1. **Net profit is the primary objective.** Business decisions should optimize for durable net profit before secondary objectives.
2. **Avoid unnecessary recurring Owner lock-in.** Owner freedom and low recurring Owner burden are operating constraints. Additional recurring Owner involvement should require a material economic justification.
3. **Prefer stronger business asset value when economics and Owner burden are otherwise comparable.** Saleability, owned intellectual property, reusable systems, and durable business assets are preferred tie-breakers.
4. **AI experimentation is an enabling environment, not a higher-order business objective.** The ability to experiment with AI and new tools is valuable when it supports profit, operating leverage, learning, or asset creation.

This normalization reconciles prior priority statements without treating Owner freedom and asset value as interchangeable concepts.

## 3. Business Structure Preference

- Concentrate on one primary business until stable profit is established.
- Multiple businesses may be added after the primary business reaches sufficient stability or when portfolio expansion is economically justified.
- Do not preserve a current business solely because it already exists.
- A switch to a different business opportunity is acceptable when evidence supports materially better expected value.
- Do not discard an already stable profitable business unnecessarily. If it can be retained with low Owner involvement through automation or delegation, preservation is preferred.

## 4. Ownership and Operating Model

- Prefer maintaining KAVORA as a one-person company for as long as it remains economically rational.
- Remaining a one-person company is not itself the final objective.
- Avoid scale models that depend primarily on employee development and people-management overhead.
- Preferred scale mechanisms, in order, are:
  1. AI
  2. Software
  3. Automation
  4. Outsourcing / contractors when justified
  5. Employment as the last resort
- Outsourcing is acceptable when financial capacity exists and expected ROI justifies it.

## 5. Owner Work Preference and Operating Constraints

### Owner involvement worth retaining

- Dialogue with AI
- Trying new tools
- Reviewing business numbers

### Owner constraints to minimize

- Sales calls
- Store visits
- Web meetings
- Dependence on borrowing
- Heavy fixed-cost structures

Asynchronous text channels such as email, DM, and chat are acceptable for Owner participation when human involvement is still necessary. Over time, these interactions should also be delegated to AI where safe and economically justified.

## 6. AI Authority Preference

- Trust in AI authority should expand through demonstrated Revenue Evidence and safe execution history.
- Evidence that AI recommendations or instructions produced actual profit is a primary trust signal.
- Where Revenue Evidence and safety controls are sufficient, the Owner is willing to delegate a broad range of decisions and execution to AI.
- Borrowing authority remains Owner-exclusive.

This section records an Owner delegation preference only. It is not an immediate Authority Grant and does not supersede existing Role Profiles, governance, Human-only Action boundaries, or approval requirements.

## 7. Capital Allocation Preference

- Be conservative with initial investment.
- Prefer small experiments before larger commitments.
- A current preferred initial experiment scale is approximately JPY 100,000 before evidence-based follow-on investment.
- Increase capital allocation only after evidence improves the expected return case.
- Losses are more acceptable when the experiment is designed to produce information that can materially improve the next profit decision.
- In the near term, prefer Cash Return to the Owner over automatic reinvestment of profits.

## 8. Asset Preference

When expected profit and operating burden are broadly comparable, prefer business models in which KAVORA owns reusable assets such as:

- Software
- Systems
- Products
- Reusable intellectual or operational assets

Preferred sequence:

`Short-term Cash Engine -> Profit and Evidence -> Larger Owned Asset`

Short-term cash generation should finance and de-risk movement toward higher-value owned assets rather than requiring speculative infrastructure investment first.

## 9. Exit Preference

- Permanent ownership of KAVORA is not a strategic requirement.
- KAVORA may be sold when terms are sufficiently attractive.
- KAVORA should be treated primarily as a business asset that produces profit, cash, and asset value rather than as a brand whose preservation is an end in itself.

## 10. Decision Continuity Guidance

Future CEO and Executive decisions should use this artifact as durable Owner context when comparing business opportunities, resource allocation, scale mechanisms, automation decisions, and exit options.

The preferred evaluation order is:

1. Expected durable net profit
2. Required recurring Owner burden and operating constraints
3. Business asset value / saleability / owned reusable assets
4. Capital efficiency and downside learning value
5. AI-enabled operating leverage and experimentation value

This order is a decision heuristic, not a substitute for case-specific evidence.

## 11. Compatibility with Existing Context

### Founding Principles

- **Profit First:** aligned. The new context makes the economic target more concrete without changing the principle.
- **Business First:** aligned. Technology and AI remain subordinate to business outcomes.
- **AI for Execution:** aligned. The Owner prefers increasingly broad AI delegation after Revenue Evidence and safety are sufficient.
- **Human for Judgment:** no governance change is made here. Conditional willingness to delegate does not itself transfer Owner-exclusive or otherwise protected authority.

### Business Portfolio

AI Company OS may support multiple Revenue Engines, while the current Owner operating preference is to establish stable profit in one primary business before expanding. These are compatible: architecture capacity does not require immediate portfolio diversification.

### Conflict Status

No direct conflict requiring Architecture or Governance modification was identified.

Potential interpretation tension between Owner freedom and business asset value has been normalized in Section 2: Owner burden is treated as an operating constraint, while asset value is a preference among economically comparable options.

## 12. Preservation Boundary

Do not add interview transcripts or personal-life details to this artifact.

Future updates should preserve only strategic business knowledge that materially affects KAVORA decisions. Changes should be versioned rather than silently rewriting prior Owner context.
