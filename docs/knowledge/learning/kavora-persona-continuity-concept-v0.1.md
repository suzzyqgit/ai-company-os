# KAVORA Persona Continuity Concept v0.1

## Observation

Role Lock fixes Identity / Mission / Authority but does not guarantee identical conversational texture across chats.

Same-chat accumulated tone, wording tendencies, and interaction style can be partially lost when a new chat starts.

## Concept

Future Persona Continuity may preserve:
- role-consistent natural voice
- interaction conventions
- stable executive communication texture

without weakening Governance, Authority Boundary, Evidence Guard, or Role Lock.

Target operating principle:

> **人格は自然、Governanceは機械的に厳密。**

- Research Only

- No change to current Role Lock semantics
