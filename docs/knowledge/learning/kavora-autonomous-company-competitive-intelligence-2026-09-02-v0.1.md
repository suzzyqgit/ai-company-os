## Robust Findings

1. Long-running / persistent agent execution is increasingly practical.
2. Agent activity and business profitability are not equivalent.
3. Adaptive Revenue Decision Loops are a critical unresolved business layer.
4. Platform-provider revenue must not be treated as proof that customer businesses are profitable.
5. Compute / model cost can destroy unit economics even when revenue growth is strong.
6. Human escalation, permission, audit, memory, and cost gates remain material.
