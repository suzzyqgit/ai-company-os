# Owner Knowledge Base Specification v1.0

## Purpose

Define the structure for preserving the Owner's long-term knowledge, decision principles, and strategic insights.

## Scope

The Owner Knowledge Base shall preserve:

- Founding Principles
- Management Philosophy
- Business Vision
- Decision Principles
- Priorities
- AI Philosophy
- Architecture Philosophy
- Strategic Insights
- Long-term Objectives

## Knowledge Sources

Knowledge may be updated only from:

- Executive Interview
- Approved Decision
- Owner Directive
- Strategic Insight Report

## Knowledge Categories

### Founding Principles

Core principles that should remain stable over time.

### Decision Principles

Rules used by the Owner to make decisions.

### Strategic Vision

Long-term direction of AI Company OS.

### Priorities

Current management priorities.

### Learning History

Chronological record of significant changes in Owner thinking.

## Update Workflow

Executive Interview

↓

Strategic Insight Report

↓

Knowledge Review

↓

Owner Knowledge Base Update

↓

Repository Registration

## Traceability

Every update shall reference:

- Executive Interview
- Strategic Insight Report
- Related Decision
- Related Case

## Governance

This specification follows:

- Knowledge Repository Standard v1.0
- Executive Learning Framework v1.0
