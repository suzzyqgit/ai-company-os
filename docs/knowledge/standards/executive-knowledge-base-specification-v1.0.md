# Executive Knowledge Base Specification v1.0

## Purpose

Define the structure for preserving and evolving executive-specific knowledge within AI Company OS.

## Scope

Each Executive Knowledge Base shall preserve:

- Role Responsibilities
- Owner Understanding
- Domain Knowledge
- Decision History
- Strategic Learnings
- Cross-Executive Learnings
- Improvement Opportunities

## Knowledge Sources

Knowledge may be updated only from:

- Executive Interview
- Strategic Dialogue
- Decision Report
- Approved Architecture
- Owner Directive

## Knowledge Categories

### Executive Responsibilities

Responsibilities assigned to the executive.

### Owner Understanding

Current understanding of the Owner's philosophy and priorities.

### Domain Knowledge

Knowledge specific to the executive's domain.

### Strategic Learnings

Insights gained through dialogue and execution.

### Decision History

Important architectural or business decisions.

## Update Workflow

Learning Activity

↓

Knowledge Review

↓

Executive Knowledge Update

↓

Repository Registration

## Traceability

Every update shall reference:

- Related Executive
- Related Report
- Related Decision
- Related Case

## Governance

This specification follows:

- Knowledge Repository Standard v1.0
- Executive Learning Framework v1.0
- Owner Knowledge Base Specification v1.0
