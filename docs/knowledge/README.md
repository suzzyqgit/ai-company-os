# Knowledge Layer

## Purpose

The Knowledge Layer stores approved company knowledge for AI Company OS.

`data/` = facts.

`knowledge/` = approved business knowledge.

Future AI roles must reference `knowledge/` before proposing strategies.

## Owner

AI Company OS Governance.

## Update Rule

Only approved knowledge may be committed here. Temporary notes, chat logs, drafts, and unreviewed opinions must not be stored in this layer.

## Source of Truth

This directory is the Source of Truth for approved business knowledge derived from verified data, decisions, standards, and role-specific operating knowledge.
